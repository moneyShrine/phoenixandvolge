Alternative Dispute Resolution
Arbitration, Mediation & Conciliation

Our approach to dispute resolution is to embark on litigation as an option of last resort. We encourage parties to settle their disputes amicably out of court. The challenges of litigating commercial disputes, particularly those arising from international contracts are enormous and may be counterproductive in the long run; even for the winning party usually because of the uncertainty and unpredictability; as to the ultimate outcome and time frame associated with litigation. Arbitration therefore has become the preferred mode of dispute resolution in commercial disputes. Arbitration is essentially a private process for the binding resolution of a dispute through decision of one or more private individuals selected by parties to the dispute. The Arbitral process may take place within the framework of an ad-hoc arrangement, where the parties devise their own procedure or within the context of established Arbitration institutions. At Phoenix & Volge we have developed the capacity and expertise in that regard and we are poised to deliver on our promise. Other forms of non-binding means of dispute resolution includes: conciliation, mediation and negotiation; which may be expedient in certain situations. We are poised to properly guide our clients through the process in a bid to achieve the best possible result.
